Quick Physics Notes

🔋 PN Junction I–V

The Shockley diode equation models current as a function of applied voltage. Temperature affects the thermal voltage, so changing temperature changes the predicted curve.

I = Iₛ [ exp(qV/nkT) − 1 ]

⚛️ Quantum particle

For an infinite one-dimensional box, allowed energies are quantized. The quantum number n determines the standing-wave pattern.

Eₙ = n²h² / 8mL²

〰️ SHM

Simple harmonic motion is periodic motion where the restoring effect is proportional to displacement. The ideal solution is sinusoidal.

x(t) = A cos(ωt)

🌡️ Heat diffusion

The heat equation describes how temperature varies with position and time. Numerical finite differences let a computer approximate its solution.

∂T/∂t = α ∂²T/∂x²

🧮 Numerical methods

Euler and Runge–Kutta methods approximate differential equations step by step. They are useful when an analytical solution is difficult.

📊 Read a graph

Always identify the axes, units, parameter values and model assumptions before interpreting a curve. Change one parameter at a time to see its effect clearly.
